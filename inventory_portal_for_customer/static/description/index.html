Inventory Portal for Customer<

Provide customers with secure, read-only visibility into product availability, stock levels, and forecasted quantities-directly from the Odoo Portal.

Highlights

  • Customer-friendly inventory view via Odoo Portal (no backend access)
  • Browse products using expandable, category-based navigation
  • Real-time visibility of Qty On Hand and Forecasted Quantity
  • Expected availability (ETA) based on incoming stock
  • Read-only by design — safe for inventory and accounting teams
  • Optional customer-specific pricing using Odoo pricelists

How to Use

  1. Grant Portal access to your customers
  2. Customers log in to the Odoo Portal
  3. Navigate to the Inventory menu
  4. Browse products by category or use the search bar

Compatibility: Odoo 18
